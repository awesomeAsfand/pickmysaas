## Why we built it

More and more customers ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI answers instead of clicking through search results. A page that those systems can't read properly doesn't get quoted. We wanted a quick, honest way to show a business where its pages stand.

## What it does

- Scores any page out of 100 across eight signals, including structured data, heading order and AI crawler access
- Needs no sign-up and no email address
- Comes with an evidence page that rates common AEO (answer engine optimisation) claims, so the advice is backed by data, not hype

## Why it matters

Snippet Tester is how we hold our own work to account. Every site we ship is checked against the same signals, in both Arabic and English.
