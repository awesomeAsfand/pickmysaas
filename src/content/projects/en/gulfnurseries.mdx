GulfNurseries is a nursery directory for the six Gulf countries that we designed and built for a client in 3 weeks, for a fixed $1,500. It lists more than 3,000 nurseries, lets parents search by city and neighbourhood on a map and contact a nursery on WhatsApp, and earns from nursery owners on paid plans: 45 of the 100 owners who have claimed a listing pay for one.

## The brief

Parents across the Gulf had no single place to find and compare nurseries. Information was scattered across social media, outdated listings and word of mouth. Our client wanted one trusted directory that covers the whole GCC, and gives nursery owners a reason to keep their listing up to date.

That meant three audiences to build for: parents who need answers quickly, nursery owners who need a reason to pay, and search engines that need to understand thousands of pages.

## What we built

### For parents

- Search by nursery name, city and neighbourhood, with results on a map
- Area pages, such as Mohammed Bin Zayed City in Abu Dhabi, that list every nursery nearby with filters for curriculum and opening hours
- Curriculum pages, such as British or Montessori nurseries in Abu Dhabi
- Nursery pages that show photos, curriculum, age groups, fees, opening hours and Google reviews
- Call and WhatsApp buttons on every listing, so a parent can arrange a visit in one tap. Parents contact the nursery directly, with no booking step in between.

### For nursery owners

- A free listing that the owner claims and keeps up to date. Every claim is checked by hand before the owner gets access.
- Featured and Premium plans, priced per country, for owners who want to stand out
- Plans paid by invoice in the local currency, by bank transfer or card, monthly or yearly, with nothing renewing automatically
- An advertising page for brands that want to reach Gulf parents

### For search engines and AI assistants

- A page structure that matches how parents search: country, then city, then neighbourhood, then nursery. In November 2026 the sitemap lists about 1,450 pages.
- Structured data on every nursery page (ChildCare, with address, map position and opening hours), so search engines read a listing as a real local business
- Lists and FAQ markup on the area pages, and breadcrumbs everywhere
- 20 parent guides on nursery licensing, starting age and choosing a nursery in each country, plus fee guides for Saudi Arabia and the UAE

## How it's built

The directory runs on Python and Django, the same stack we use for [our websites](/services/websites/) and online stores. Django suits a directory: thousands of listings, an admin where the client checks claims and edits nurseries, and pages generated from the data rather than written one by one. The maps use Leaflet, an open-source map library, and Cloudflare serves the site.

Because the code is custom, nothing limits the page structure or the plans. There's no directory plugin deciding what a listing page looks like, or how the owner plans work.

## Results

As of November 2026:

- **3 weeks** from the start of the project to launch
- **$1,500**, the fixed price of the build
- **100 nursery owners** have claimed their free listing
- **45 of them** pay for a plan, which is 45% of the owners who claimed a listing
- **More than 3,000 nurseries** listed across all six GCC countries

Listings are free to claim, and paid plans give owners more visibility, so the directory earns from the nurseries that want to stand out, not from parents.

## What it doesn't do yet

The directory is in English. Many Gulf parents search in Arabic, so an Arabic version would reach more of them; that's a decision for the client. Parents also can't book a visit on the site itself. They call or message the nursery, which is how most families already do it.
