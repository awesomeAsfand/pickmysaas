## The brief

Parents across the Gulf had no single place to find and compare nurseries. Information was scattered across social media, outdated listings and word of mouth. Our client wanted one trusted directory that covers the whole GCC and gives nursery owners a reason to keep their listing up to date.

## What we built

- A directory of more than 3,000 nurseries in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman and Bahrain
- Search by city and neighbourhood, with results on a map
- Nursery pages that compare photos, curriculum, age groups, fees, opening hours and Google reviews
- Call and WhatsApp buttons on every listing, so parents can book a visit in one tap
- Free listings that owners can claim, with paid plans and advertising on top
- Parent guides on licensing, starting age and choosing a nursery, written to rank in search

## Why it matters

The platform pays for itself: listings are free to claim, and the paid plans give owners more visibility. It is the kind of product we build for the region — local in how people search and contact businesses, and built to grow across six countries.
