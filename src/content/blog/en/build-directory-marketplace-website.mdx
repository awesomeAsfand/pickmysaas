**The short answer:** start with a directory, not a marketplace. List the businesses yourself, let owners claim their listing for free, earn from paid plans that give them more visibility, and let customers contact businesses directly on the phone or WhatsApp. It's quicker to build, you don't handle anyone's money, and it proves people will use the platform before you add bookings or payments.

That's how [GulfNurseries](/projects/gulfnurseries/) works: a directory of more than 3,000 nurseries across the six Gulf countries that we built for a client. By November 2026, 100 nursery owners had claimed their listing and 45 of them paid for a plan. This guide uses it as the worked example.

## Directory or marketplace?

| | Directory | Marketplace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What customers do** | Find a business and contact it directly | Book or buy on your platform |
| **How you earn** | Paid plans, advertising, lead fees | Commission on each sale or booking |
| **Who handles payment** | The business | You, then you pay the business |
| **Disputes and refunds** | Between customer and business | Often yours to handle |
| **What it takes to build** | Listings, search, a claim process, plans | All that, plus bookings, payments, payouts, reviews |
| **Extra approvals** | Rarely | Possibly, if you collect money for others |

A directory can become a marketplace later, once you know which businesses and customers use it. Going the other way is much harder.

## How a directory makes money

- **Paid visibility plans.** Listings are free; businesses pay to appear higher, add more photos or get a badge. GulfNurseries uses this, with Featured and Premium plans priced per country.
- **Advertising.** Brands that want to reach your audience pay for space. GulfNurseries has an advertising page for brands that want to reach Gulf parents.
- **Lead fees.** Businesses pay for each enquiry. This works when enquiries are valuable and easy to count.
- **Commission.** You take a share of each booking or sale. This turns your directory into a marketplace, with everything that comes with it.

Start with one or two. Paid plans are usually the easiest first step: simple to explain, simple to invoice, and they don't depend on tracking every customer.

## Where the first listings come from

A directory with no listings has no visitors, and a directory with no visitors can't attract listings. Break that loop yourself:

1. **Add the businesses yourself first,** from public information: name, area, phone number, opening hours. On GulfNurseries, most of the 3,000+ nurseries are listed without their owners having claimed them yet; 100 have claimed so far.
2. **Let owners claim their listing for free,** so they can add photos and correct details.
3. **Check every claim by hand** before giving access. GulfNurseries asks for proof such as an email at the nursery's own domain or a trade licence.
4. **Offer paid plans once the directory brings visitors,** not before. Owners pay when they can see parents are looking.

## What to build first

Build what makes the directory useful on day one, and nothing more:

- search by name, city and area, with results on a map
- a page for each listing, with the details customers compare
- call and WhatsApp buttons on every listing
- a free claim process for owners, and an admin to check claims and edit listings
- a plans page, with payment by invoice to start
- a page structure that search engines can follow (see below)

Leave for later: in-site booking, online payments, a reviews system of your own, and mobile apps. Add each one when customers ask for it, not because competitors have it.

## Getting found on Google

Most directory traffic comes from search, so plan the structure before you build:

- **Pages that match how people search:** country, then city, then area, then the listing. GulfNurseries' sitemap lists about 1,450 pages built this way.
- **Structured data on every listing,** using the right schema.org type for the business, with address, map position and opening hours.
- **Guides that answer customers' questions.** GulfNurseries has guides on nursery licensing, starting age, choosing a nursery and fees, by country.
- **Arabic as well as English,** if your customers search in both.

## Taking payments in a marketplace

If you move to bookings or sales on the platform, the money flows through you. You'll need a payment gateway that supports marketplaces, often called split payments, so each payment is divided between the business and your commission. Ask your gateway what approvals that needs in your country. Our guide to [payment gateways in the Gulf](/blog/payment-gateway-integration-gulf/) covers the gateways and what they ask for.

## Mistakes that sink new platforms

- **Launching empty,** and hoping businesses will add themselves
- **Charging owners before the platform brings them visitors**
- **Building a mobile app first,** before anyone uses the website
- **Too many features at launch,** which delays it by months
- **No checks on who claims a listing,** which destroys trust quickly
